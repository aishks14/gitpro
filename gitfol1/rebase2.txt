Purpose
------------------------------------------
Second practice file for "git rebase". Commit it on a branch,
add a new commit on master, then rebase the branch so its
commits sit on top of master in one straight line.

------------------------------------------
Merge vs Rebase
------------------------------------------
Merge  : joins two histories with a merge commit, keeps the fork.
Rebase : replays your commits on the new base, history stays linear.
